Ongoing

ongoingECDC · commercial research contract

Evaluation of Large Language Models for Automated HAI Case Definition Matching for Surveillance

Hospital patient records are long, free-text documents. We use large language models (open-weight models run on premises, and commercial ones, under one shared configuration) to extract over a hundred clinical variables per infection episode, then apply the ECDC case definitions for healthcare-associated infections with a deterministic, auditable rule engine. Results are compared with physicians’ independent labels, case by case.

  • large language models
  • clinical NLP
  • ECDC case definitions
  • on-premise AI

FISHUB · Fish Identification Software Hub

Selling one fish species as another is the most frequent fraud in seafood. FISHUB built an objective, field-usable detector: image analysis and machine learning identify the species from a photo, catching morphological differences that are hard to see with the naked eye and without the cost of DNA analysis.

partners Istituto Zooprofilattico Sperimentale del Piemonte Liguria e Valle d'Aosta · Politecnico di Torino · University of Salford

OK il pesce è giusto

A mobile app for fish forgery detection built for EXPO 2015 with the Istituto Zooprofilattico: photograph the fish, pick the declared species, and let the server’s classifier say whether the label is honest.

IBIO OSDD Research Center

A cloud framework for open drug-discovery networks able to run industrial-standard pharmaceutical projects at reduced cost, piloted on genetic risk factors of Parkinson’s disease in about one hundred subjects.

MIND ·

Engineering physiologically and pathologically relevant organ models for the investigation of age-related diseases

Ageing is not just a cellular process: MIND built organ-level models of age-related degeneration, combining in-vitro experiments with computational models, with eleven Italian partners.
