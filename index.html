Recent work

two things we are excited about right now
epigeneticsBest Paper · IWBBIO 2026

Reading early signs of breast cancer in DNA methylation

A biologically constrained panel of CpG sites that classifies breast field cancerisation: the molecular changes in tissue that still looks healthy but is already on its way. Elisabetta Roviera’s thesis work, with Vincenzo Sandro Gambino of the Rivoli hospital.

Read the paper
LLMs for healthwith ECDC

Language models that read hospital records for infection surveillance

Large language models, open and commercial, extract more than a hundred clinical variables from free-text patient records; a transparent rule engine then applies the ECDC case definitions for healthcare-associated bloodstream infections. Validated against physicians’ own labels.

What we work on

keywords from our polito.it profiles

Gene regulatory networks

Boolean and multi-level Petri-net models of how genes, transcription factors and microRNAs regulate each other, and what their attractors say about cell fate.

Biological databases & data integration

Merging dozens of public -omics sources into one queryable graph. theRING integrates 38 of them into a single vocabulary of regulatory interactions.

Deep learning for health

Neural networks for protein function prediction, circulating microRNA signatures, epigenetic markers and language models that read clinical records.

Biostatistics

Multivariate statistics and systematic analyses of large public datasets, the way we found the miRNA Pathway Protection Loop motif.

Built for exploratory analysis, so it needs no expression data.Cytoscape 3 pluginReNERegulatory Network Enhancer. Imports pathways from KEGG and Reactome (or any OWL/XML Cytoscape accepts), merges them, and enriches them with transcriptional, post-transcriptional and translational regulation.Cytoscape 2.8 pluginFunMODMines undirected protein–protein networks for sub-networks tied to biological pathways, scoring three topological features of each module to describe the role of every protein in it.C++ simulatorBNToolkitSimulates and analyses Boolean gene regulatory networks, computes attractors and state spaces, and models post-transcriptional regulation by microRNAs.
