PhD and MS in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino. Works on complex biological network analysis, data fusion and statistical data analysis; author of theRING database and of the ReNE and CyTransfinder Cytoscape plugins. Teaches Bioinformatics in the MSc programme and serves on the programme and organising committees of several IEEE conferences.
